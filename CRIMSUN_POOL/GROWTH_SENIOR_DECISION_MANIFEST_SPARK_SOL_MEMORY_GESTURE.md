**PHASE 1 — Prove the picture is the state.** A failed remember draws no star and no motif. A successful remember of one new key draws that key’s birth motif once. Exit: the row is in the store before the motif starts.

**PHASE 2 — Carry the grammar.** Recall of one to three notes plays each star’s recall motif while the existing single fly runs. Small talk calls no camera method and no motif.

**PHASE 3 — Unproven uniqueness.** The motif table is finite. Same key, same motif, including after reload. Two keys do not share a motif unless the table is smaller than the sky, in which case they may share a shape and must still differ by dye. Reduced motion is a filament hold, not a silent skip of the status line.

**PHASE 4 —** `?mute=1` keeps the motif. A reload that is not exactly one new star plays none.
